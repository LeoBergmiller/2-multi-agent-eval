# Same-cause readmission rate

The share of eligible index inpatient admissions followed within 30 days by a
readmission **for the same or a clinically related condition**, judged by the diagnosis
recorded on both encounters.

## When this is the right metric

Condition-specific programmes — heart failure, COPD, pneumonia — where the question is
whether a *particular* condition was managed to discharge, not whether the patient
returned for any reason at all.

## Why the default is all-cause instead

All-cause is the standard for quality measurement precisely because same-cause is
gameable and fragile: it depends on diagnosis coding, on how "clinically related" is
defined, and on which of several diagnosis fields is consulted. A readmission for a
complication of the index treatment is arguably related and frequently coded as
something else. Narrowing to same-cause always produces a lower rate, and how much lower
depends on coding practice rather than on care.

## Not to be confused with

[[readmission_30day]], which counts a readmission for **any** cause. "All-cause" is not
a qualifier that can be dropped — it is the substance of the definition.
