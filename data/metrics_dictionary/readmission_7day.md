# 7-day readmission rate

The share of eligible index inpatient admissions followed by an unplanned inpatient
readmission within **7 days** of discharge.

Same construction as [[readmission_30day]] with a 7-day window.

## When this is the right metric

**Early readmission** is a discharge-process measure rather than a care-quality one. A
return within a week points at premature discharge, inadequate discharge planning,
medication reconciliation failures, or missing follow-up arrangements — things the
discharging team controls directly.

Because the window is short, the metric is far more specific to the index stay and far
less affected by community factors, but the counts are small and the rate is noisy at
low volumes. Report the numerator alongside the rate.

## Not to be confused with

[[readmission_30day]], the standard quality window. A 7-day rate is roughly a third of
the 30-day rate on comparable populations, so substituting it produces a number that
looks like a dramatic improvement.
